About Me

I'm a software engineer who works across the stack — from integrating enterprise SAP EDI pipelines to designing service-to-service communication with gRPC, and building responsive React interfaces on top. My focus has largely been on Clean Architecture backends in .NET, wiring together REST APIs, gRPC microservices, NATS-based messaging, and WebSockets for real-time features.

I've shipped production systems for clients in the GCC region (Saudi Arabia and Oman), working on everything from CRM platforms and HR management tools to invoice/procurement systems and budget & asset management software — several of them integrated directly with SAP.
